# Revision Record

This record lists every change made to the book after its first publication as version 1.0. It exists for the reason given in §3.7: a revision may change the model, but it may not erase the record of what an earlier version said. Pre-publication revisions (assemblies v0.9.x) are recorded separately in the Book Edition Amendment Record.

**Version numbers.** A change in the third place (1.0.1, 1.0.2) corrects text without altering any claim: typographical errors, citations, dates and links. A change in the second place (1.1, 1.2) updates facts or changes the status of a claim, for example after new evidence or a recount. A change to the architecture itself, meaning its objects, mapping, propositions or boundaries, requires a new edition.

**Status changes.** An entry that changes a claim’s standing says so in its last column, and the change is mirrored in the Claims and Status Register. No claim’s status changes without an entry here.

**Where to find the current record.** The online version at [https://champaignmagazine.github.io/on-gradual-agi/revision-record/](https://champaignmagazine.github.io/on-gradual-agi/revision-record/) is maintained between printings. Each printing reproduces the record as of its date.
