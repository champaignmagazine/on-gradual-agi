# Claims and Status Register

This compact register selects claims and evidentiary boundaries that a reader needs to carry across chapters. It is not a longer provenance ledger in abbreviated form. Definitions, architectural admission, formal consequences, and empirical outcomes have different kinds of standing; the columns do not form a confidence ranking.

**How to read status.** Canonical means admitted at the specified architectural level, not universally empirically validated. High-confidence candidate is below canonical admission. Provisional remains subject to a stated unresolved burden. Untested means the required test was not exercised. Non-operable means current evidence cannot execute the specified test. Not adjudicated means the available design and evidence do not decide the relevant classification. Finite non-refutation records that an exercised falsifier did not trigger; it is not proof. These distinctions must not be replaced by one score.

**Additional status language.** Proposed governance design identifies a specified arrangement awaiting comparative testing. Antecedent hypothesis preserves an earlier conjecture without promoting it to an established necessity claim. Architectural representation specifies objects and relations without claiming an identified empirical function. Formal-conditional consequences follow under stated model assumptions. Conditional tools apply only when their assumptions are met. Structural sign retained preserves a directional relationship while calibration remains open. Documented non-veto association sequences record bounded observed pathways, not causal identification. Unexercised means the required antecedent or test occasion did not occur. Not identified means the analysis does not establish the specified effect or parameter. Executable means a test is specified and can be run; measurement-limited qualifies its evidentiary reach. Illustrated and direct case support describe the evidence supplied, without asserting prevalence. Analytically retained is an analytical disposition; not finitely adjudicable means a finite test cannot settle the stated claim. Probed, not falsified reports a probe’s result without confirmation. Validation unresolved and calibration open identify unfinished reliability or parameter-estimation burdens.

**Claim types.** The status vocabulary applies by claim type. Evidence statuses apply only to empirical propositions; admission tiers apply to synthetic principles; derivation applies to formal results. An admission tier such as High-confidence candidate records how far a principle has passed the inherited admission criteria. It is not an empirical confidence level.
