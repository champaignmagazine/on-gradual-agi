# Full Notation Guide

This is the fuller reader reference paired with the front-matter Notation Quick Reference. It covers book-level and repeatedly reused symbols and relations. Local one-off notation remains defined in its chapter; “Full” does not mean a complete index of every symbol in every source paper.

Indices are local to their expressions: g identifies a group, d a domain, t a time or observation period, and i an actor, evaluator, or segment as specified in the chapter. Identical letters in different local roles do not establish identical objects. Subscripts and Greek letters must be preserved in final typesetting.

Realization and Exposure

The central mapping has exactly four arguments:

$$R_{g}(t) = \mu_{t}\left( P(t),Av(t),Ex_{g}(t),Z_{d}(t) \right).$$

It is not a sum or a fixed sequence. Optimization, governance, Participation, epistemic discipline, and feedback can affect existing objects or relations without becoming fifth inputs.

**Similar symbols, distinct roles.** $S_{t}$ is CFS Slope; ${St}_{t}$ is Underlying State and $St_{g}(t)$ a standing institution. $R_{g}(t)$ is realized state; ${Rp}_{t}$ is Representation. The inherited joint-policy space $\Pi$ is distinct from the Participatory Configuration $\Pi_{r}\left\lbrack t_{0},t_{1} \right\rbrack$; Chapter 6’s reader notation uses $\mathcal{T}$. Chapter 4’s domain conditions $Z_{d}(t)$ are distinct from Chapter 6’s local quantity $Z_{j}(t)$ constrained by Absorption Capacity. Participatory Consequence $C_{r}$ (or consequence class $C_{rj}$) is distinct from CFS Ceiling $C_{t}$. Indices, upright letters, and chapter context preserve these distinctions.

For CFS, β = 1 is the parity case, β = 2 is the marginal oscillatory boundary, and β \> 2 is divergent under the minimal recurrence. Those boundaries do not automatically transfer to continuous-time, nonlinear, or stochastic alternatives. A fixed point is not automatically a stable attractor.

For a positive gap, $\beta\  > \ 1$ crosses the prior-period Ceiling. Crossing the contemporaneous Ceiling additionally requires $(\beta\  - \ 1)G_{t}\  > \ \alpha$. Oscillation around a positive fixed-point gap does not itself establish that crossing or demonstrated overtrust (§5.7).

The Participation profile is read in the order RO → FP → AD. Its ordering is an editorial convention, not a temporal or causal sequence. The five-term epistemic separation marks conceptual non-identity. Temporal arrows in a return-path representation carry evidentiary burdens; their display does not establish feedback, recursion, or RSI.
